# Opening hook review

Judge the opening by what the reader can understand and want within the first
beats. Do not force a loud hook when the selected genre calls for quiet
suspense; make the promise legible through concrete detail instead.

## Required context

1. Call `read_writing_context` for the book promise, genre, chapter goal, and
   protagonist state.
2. Call `read_draft` for the opening passage or chapter.
3. Preserve the chosen viewpoint, genre, setting, and planned reveal boundary.

## Review rules

- Establish a concrete situation, protagonist presence, and pressure quickly.
- Surface the story's distinctive promise through an event, choice, anomaly,
  relationship, or risk rather than explanation.
- Delay background that does not affect the opening decision or tension.
- Make the reader's next question specific and answerable through progression.
- Avoid manufactured mystery, empty spectacle, and a prologue that postpones
  the actual story without a clear reason.

## Output

Report the first effective hook, the missing promise or pressure, and the
smallest repair. If rewriting was requested, rewrite only the opening scope and
do not change later plot facts.
