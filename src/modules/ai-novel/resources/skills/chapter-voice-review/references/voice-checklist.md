# Voice checklist

- [ ] `read_writing_context` was called before judging the prose.
- [ ] The target passage or chapter was read with its scene boundary intact.
- [ ] Narrator person, tense, distance, diction, and rhythm were checked.
- [ ] Focalization does not expose unavailable knowledge or unexplained inference.
- [ ] Tone fits the genre, scene purpose, and established book baseline.
- [ ] Sentence rhythm, paragraph shape, repetition, idiom, and terminology are intentional.
- [ ] Character speech and interior language remain distinguishable from narration.
- [ ] A deliberate state-driven voice change was not falsely marked as drift.
- [ ] Findings are evidence-backed and limited to expression/viewpoint concerns.
- [ ] No Contract, MainLine, Draft, or other durable state was modified.
