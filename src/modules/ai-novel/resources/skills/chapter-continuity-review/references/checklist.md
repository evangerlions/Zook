# Continuity checklist

- [ ] `read_writing_context` was called before making a judgment.
- [ ] The target draft was read in full or the reviewed passage was bounded.
- [ ] Canonical facts, names, locations, objects, rules, and abilities match.
- [ ] Timeline, travel, injuries, recoveries, and character knowledge match.
- [ ] Character goals, motives, relationships, secrets, and emotions have causes.
- [ ] The chapter stays inside the active goal, beat, and future-milestone boundary.
- [ ] Important actions have triggers and consequences; reveals have evidence.
- [ ] Every finding points to draft evidence and an established source.
- [ ] Unknown evidence is reported as unknown rather than filled with invented canon.
- [ ] No Contract, MainLine, Draft, or other durable state was modified.
