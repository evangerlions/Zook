# Chapter ending review

The ending must land on a changed situation, not merely announce that the
chapter is over. Do not resolve a long-term thread just to manufacture closure.

## Required context

1. Call `read_writing_context` for the chapter goal, MainLine, and open threads.
2. Call `read_draft` for the complete target chapter.
3. Preserve the intended ending boundary and the user's chosen outcome.

## Review rules

- The final movement must contain a concrete change in information, action,
  relationship, resource, danger, choice, or cost.
- Prefer a specific unresolved question, consequence, choice, or threat over a
  generic promise that something exciting will happen.
- Let the hook grow from the scene's cause and character choices; do not add an
  unrelated shock.
- Remove final paragraphs that summarize the lesson or explain the reader's
  emotion.
- Check that the next chapter has a real reason to begin from this ending.

## Output

Return the ending's current landing, its pressure gap, and one or two repair
options. If rewriting was requested, return the revised ending only. Do not
write durable story state.
