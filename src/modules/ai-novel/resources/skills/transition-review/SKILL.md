# Transition review

Treat a transition as a bridge carrying forward a live element, not as a bare
location label. Do not use this Skill to redesign the chapter's plot.

## Required context

1. Call `read_writing_context` for the active chapter intent and open threads.
2. Call `read_draft` for the preceding and following passages.
3. Preserve the chapter plan, event order, viewpoint, and established facts.

## Review rules

- Identify what the previous scene leaves active: action, object, question,
  emotion, sound, image, or consequence.
- Make the new time, space, or viewpoint legible without dumping background.
- Carry at least one meaningful element across the cut or deliberately explain
  why it is interrupted.
- Keep memories, dreams, montage, and viewpoint changes readable from context.
- Ensure the first beat after the transition changes the situation or advances
  the unresolved pressure.

## Output

Describe the broken bridge and the smallest repair. If rewriting was requested,
rewrite only the transition and its immediate landing. Do not use write tools.
